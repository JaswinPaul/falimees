Our Story

We Believe Students
Deserve a Real Home

Not just a bed in a building — but a place that supports them, connects them, and takes care of the basics so they can focus on becoming who they're meant to be.

How It Started

Built From a Real Need

Falimees was started by people who understood firsthand what student life away from home looks like — the loneliness, the logistical stress, the search for decent food and a clean space to study.

We saw that most student accommodation in Kerala focused only on providing beds. Nobody was thinking about the full experience — the community, the services, the feeling of being cared for.

So we built Falimees. Not a hostel. Not a PG. A student home — with everything a student genuinely needs to thrive.

🎯
